## It's not 2009 anymore.

Okay, times change. Technology changes. Sometimes it's okay to stay where you are at, within a given discipline or technology. You can still paint with oil or acrylics, or you can use a digital device to "paint" the same way. 

I realized a few months ago that the tech to design websites is changing. Rapidly. VERY rapidly. I knew that we were going to have to do some research into how we were doing things and decide if we stayed the course, or moved into a new way of doing things. I chose the new way. It will debut soon and then I can train my staff. Stay tuned.
